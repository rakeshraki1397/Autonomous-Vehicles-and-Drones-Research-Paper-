## Purpose

This paper compares autonomous ground vehicles and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs or drones) across artificial intelligence, perception, localization, navigation, motion planning, control, applications, limitations, and future research directions.

It is a **literature-based comparative study**, not an experimental research paper. It does not claim new experimental measurements, survey responses, or original performance results.
